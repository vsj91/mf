Technical background

Vinay is a software developer with experience building Java-based applications and web services. PlanSIP applies that engineering background to transparent financial calculators, historical NAV analysis and explainable tool design.

What this background does—and does not—mean

Software-development experience supports the implementation and review of calculations, data handling and product behaviour. It is not presented as a professional investment-advisory credential.

Vinay is not presented on PlanSIP as a SEBI-registered investment adviser, mutual fund distributor or asset-management professional. PlanSIP does not provide personalized investment recommendations.

How content is produced

PlanSIP articles are written around the site's actual tools, calculations, user questions and common interpretation mistakes. Core Indian mutual-fund concepts are checked against primary sources such as SEBI and AMFI where appropriate.

AI may assist with drafting or explanation, but AI output is not treated as a source. See the Editorial Policy and Methodology.

Corrections and accountability

Technical and factual issues can be reported through Contact & Corrections. The public project history is available in the PlanSIP GitHub repository.

Why the identity is shown

Finance-related content should make it clear who created and maintains it. This page gives readers a direct place to understand the creator's relevant background and the limits of that background.
