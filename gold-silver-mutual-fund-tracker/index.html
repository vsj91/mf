What is being compared

PlanSIP searches scheme names and available NAV histories to identify gold/silver-related funds. Scheme structures can differ, so verify the exact underlying exposure and official documents.

Tracking matters

A fund's return can differ from a headline metal index/spot price because of expenses, tracking difference, cash, fund structure and timing.

Gold and silver are not identical

Silver has more industrial-demand sensitivity, while gold is often studied more heavily as a monetary/diversification asset. Historical volatility can differ significantly.

No forecast claim

Past NAV performance is descriptive. The tool does not forecast metal prices or tell the user what allocation should be held.

Example: a recent winner

If silver funds outperform gold funds over a short period, that does not prove silver is the better long-term allocation. Compare a longer history, drawdowns and the role the asset is supposed to play in the portfolio.

How PlanSIP keeps this transparent

Inputs, assumptions and limitations are shown rather than hidden behind a single recommendation. Historical data and estimated future returns are treated differently. A screen or score is a research aid, not a buy signal.
