Privacy Policy

This page explains what the public PlanSIP website stores or sends when you use calculators, analytics and optional AI features.

No PlanSIP account required

The public calculators and learning pages do not require a PlanSIP user profile.

Browser storage

PlanSIP may use localStorage/sessionStorage for preferences, temporary tool state or cached public fund data. You can clear this through your browser.

Analytics

Google Analytics may collect standard usage/device information according to Google's policies to help understand aggregate site usage.

Advertising

Google AdSense may be used after approval. Google/advertising partners may use cookies or similar technologies according to applicable consent, privacy and advertising settings. Advertising does not change PlanSIP calculation logic.

AI requests

When you actively use an AI feature, your question and relevant PlanSIP result context may be sent to the configured AI service. Do not enter sensitive account credentials or financial identifiers in AI chat.

Third-party data/services

PlanSIP may use MFapi.in for mutual fund data, Google services for analytics/ads/translation, and Cloudflare services for configured AI/API functionality. Each provider operates under its own policies.
