Privacy Policy

This policy explains what PlanSIP stores, what third-party services may collect, and how advertising and analytics work.

Information we collect

PlanSIP is a static website. It does not require login and does not run its own backend database. The calculators may store limited preferences or cached mutual fund data in your browser using localStorage to make the site faster.

Examples include selected language, cached MFapi search results, and cached NAV analysis. This data stays in your browser unless you clear site data.

Google Analytics

PlanSIP uses Google Analytics to understand general website usage, such as page views and device/browser patterns. This helps improve performance and content. Google Analytics may use cookies or similar technologies according to Google policies.

Google AdSense and cookies

PlanSIP uses Google AdSense. Google and its partners may use cookies to serve ads, limit ad frequency, detect fraud, personalize ads where allowed, and measure ad performance.

Third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites. Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.

Third-party services

  • MFapi.in is used for mutual fund search and NAV history.
  • Google Analytics is used for website analytics.
  • Google AdSense is used for advertising after approval.
  • Google Translate may be loaded only when a non-English language is selected.

Your choices

You can clear browser localStorage/cookies, use browser privacy controls, opt out of personalized ads through Google settings, or use the site without selecting non-English translation.
