Loan Freedom Calculator: Compare Loan Balance With an Assumed SIP Corpus

The Loan Freedom tool does not tell you to invest instead of prepaying. It lets you see how a loan amortization path and a separate investment scenario might evolve under the assumptions you enter.

Loan side

The tool uses outstanding principal, interest rate, EMI and remaining period to estimate the loan path. Actual lender calculations and rate changes can differ.

Investment side

The side corpus uses a monthly contribution and assumed return. That return is uncertain and should be stress-tested rather than treated as guaranteed.

Why crossing the loan balance is not automatic freedom

Even if an assumed investment corpus becomes larger than the estimated loan balance, tax, exit timing, market conditions, liquidity needs and actual loan terms still matter.

Use conservative comparisons

Compare multiple investment returns and, where relevant, a rate increase on the loan. A plan that works only under one optimistic combination deserves caution.

Example: 8.5% loan versus 12% assumed SIP

It is incorrect to call the difference a guaranteed 3.5% advantage. The loan interest is contractual (subject to rate terms); the 12% is only a market-return assumption. The comparison is risk-adjusted, not simple subtraction.

How PlanSIP keeps this transparent

Inputs, assumptions and limitations are shown rather than hidden behind a single recommendation. Historical data and estimated future returns are treated differently. A screen or score is a research aid, not a buy signal.
