Learn

Plain-English meanings for the numbers shown in this tool.

CAGR

Compound annual growth rate shows the smoothed yearly growth between two NAV points. It is historical, not a forecast.

SIP

A systematic investment plan invests a fixed amount regularly. This tool reconstructs historical units bought using past NAVs.

NAV

Net asset value is the per-unit value of a mutual fund. Your units multiplied by latest NAV gives current value.

Volatility

Volatility measures how much monthly returns moved around. Lower volatility usually means a smoother ride.

Drawdown

Maximum drawdown estimates the biggest historical fall from a previous high NAV to a later low NAV.

Direct vs Regular

Direct plans are bought without distributor commission and often have lower expense ratios. Regular plans include distributor commission.

Growth vs IDCW

Growth plans keep gains invested in the fund. IDCW plans may distribute income, so NAV return comparison can be less straightforward.

Fund Score

The score combines historical return, consistency, volatility, drawdown, and available history. It is an analytical shortlist score, not advice.

Loan Freedom

Freedom Goals

These calculators estimate time to a target corpus from current investments, SIP amount, and assumed returns. Results are planning estimates.

Risk reminder

Market funds can lose value. Category fit and history help analysis, but they cannot remove uncertainty.
