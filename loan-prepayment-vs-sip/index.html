Loan prepayment vs SIP

Should you prepay a loan or invest separately? This guide explains the trade-offs without giving personal advice.

Open PlanSIP toolsStudy mutual funds

The basic question

Loan prepayment reduces outstanding principal and can reduce interest cost. Investing through SIP keeps money invested separately but introduces market risk.

Why prepayment feels safer

Prepaying a loan can provide a clearer mathematical benefit when the loan interest rate is known. It can also reduce stress for people who prefer lower debt.

Why some people invest separately

Some investors prefer keeping liquidity and investing for potentially higher long-term returns. But the result is uncertain and can be affected by market falls.

Important factors

Compare loan rate, expected investment return, tax impact, emergency fund, job stability, exit load, investment horizon, lender charges, and peace of mind.

How to use PlanSIP

The Loan Freedom calculator is a study model. It estimates possible loan closure timing using assumptions. It is not advice to invest, prepay, or avoid prepayment.
