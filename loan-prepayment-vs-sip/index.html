Loan Prepayment vs SIP: Compare the Trade-Off, Not Just the Returns

“Prepay the loan or invest the extra money?” cannot be answered responsibly by comparing one loan rate with one optimistic market-return assumption. The certainty of interest saved and the uncertainty of investment returns are different.

Written for: Indian mutual fund beginners   •   Last reviewed: 18 August 2026

Prepayment has a known mathematical benefit

Subject to loan terms and charges, reducing principal can reduce future interest. The effective benefit is linked to the loan rate and remaining amortization.

SIP return is uncertain

A SIP into a market-linked fund can outperform the loan rate, underperform it or lose value over a relevant period. A 12% calculator assumption is not a promised 12% investment return.

Liquidity changes the answer

Money used to prepay debt may become harder to access. Keeping sufficient emergency liquidity can matter more than optimizing a spreadsheet outcome.

Tax and loan features matter

Potential tax treatment, prepayment rules, floating versus fixed rate, remaining tenure and personal circumstances can change the trade-off. Verify current rules rather than relying on a generic article.

Use PlanSIP as a scenario test

The Loan Freedom Calculator lets you compare loan balance progression with a separate assumed-return side corpus. Test conservative as well as optimistic assumptions.
