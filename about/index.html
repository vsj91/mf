About PlanSIP

Simple finance tools for Indian investors

PlanSIP helps beginners study SIPs, mutual funds, corpus goals, loan freedom, and gold or silver fund history with clearer language and transparent calculations.

What PlanSIP does

PlanSIP is an educational website for people who want to understand mutual fund data before making financial decisions. The site includes a SIP calculator, mutual fund finder, historical SIP simulation, compare funds tool, loan freedom calculator, first crore calculator, corpus calculator, and gold/silver mutual fund tracker.

The mutual fund tools use public MFapi.in data where available. Returns are calculated from historical NAV data and are shown for learning and comparison only.

What PlanSIP does not do

  • PlanSIP does not provide investment advice.
  • PlanSIP does not recommend buying, selling, or holding any mutual fund.
  • PlanSIP does not guarantee returns or predict future performance.
  • PlanSIP is not a SEBI-registered investment adviser.

Our goal

The goal is to make finance concepts easier to understand: CAGR, SIP, NAV, volatility, drawdown, Direct vs Regular plans, Growth vs IDCW, loan payoff planning, and long-term corpus planning.
