About PlanSIP

Tools that show the assumptions behind the answer

PlanSIP is an independent educational and analytical platform for Indian users who want to study SIPs, mutual fund history, risk and long-term financial scenarios without hiding everything behind a single “best” label.

What PlanSIP is

PlanSIP combines transparent financial calculators, historical NAV research, risk-aware fund screening and AI-assisted explanations. The platform is independently developed and maintained, with the goal of making the assumptions, calculations and limitations behind each result easier to understand.

Why PlanSIP exists

Many finance pages give a formula or a list without enough context. PlanSIP combines the formula with interactive tools such as an assumption-based SIP calculator, historical SIP Replay, goal/risk-based Fund Finder, fund comparison, loan scenarios, corpus planning and gold/silver fund history.

Original PlanSIP features

  • Historical SIP Replay using available NAV observations.
  • Fund screening that considers return plus historical risk and consistency context.
  • Loan Freedom scenario comparison.
  • First-crore and corpus planning with contribution assumptions.
  • AI Assistant and AI Perspective used to explain—not replace—the underlying numbers.

How results should be used

PlanSIP is designed for education and research. It does not execute investments or provide personalized buy, sell or hold advice. Historical data, scores and AI explanations are research aids, not guarantees of future performance.

Transparency

The calculation and data approach is documented in the Methodology. Content standards, AI use and corrections are documented in the Editorial Policy.

Connect with PlanSIP

Important limitation

PlanSIP is not a SEBI-registered investment adviser or mutual fund distributor. Before making an investment decision, verify scheme information from official sources and consider your own risk profile, goals and suitability.
