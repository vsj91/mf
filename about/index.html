About PlanSIP

Tools that show the assumptions behind the answer

PlanSIP is an independent educational project for Indian users who want to study SIPs, mutual fund history, risk and long-term financial scenarios without hiding everything behind a single “best” label.

Who creates and maintains PlanSIP

PlanSIP is created and maintained by Vinay S Jain, a software developer responsible for the product design, implementation, calculator logic and ongoing content maintenance.

His software-development background is relevant to building and reviewing the tools, but it is not presented as an investment-advisory credential. PlanSIP is not a SEBI-registered investment adviser or mutual fund distributor.

Why PlanSIP exists

Many finance pages give a formula or a list. PlanSIP combines the formula with interactive tools: an assumption-based SIP calculator, historical SIP Replay, goal/risk-based Fund Finder, fund comparison, loan scenarios, corpus planning and gold/silver fund history.

Original PlanSIP features

  • Historical SIP Replay using available NAV observations.
  • Fund screening that considers return plus historical risk/consistency context.
  • Loan Freedom scenario comparison.
  • First-crore and corpus planning with contribution assumptions.
  • AI Assistant and AI Perspective used to explain—not replace—the numbers.

Transparency

The calculation/data approach is documented in the Methodology. Content standards, AI use and corrections are documented in the Editorial Policy.

Public project

PlanSIP is maintained in the public vsj91/mf GitHub repository, which provides a visible implementation/change trail and issue channel.

Important limitation

PlanSIP does not execute investments or provide personalized buy/sell/hold advice. Historical data and scores are research aids. Official scheme documents and personal suitability still matter.
