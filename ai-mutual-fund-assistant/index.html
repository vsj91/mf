AI Assistant

Ask questions about PlanSIP calculations and results. The AI Assistant helps explain SIP estimates, fund comparisons, goals, corpus calculations, salary planning and other PlanSIP outputs in simpler language.

AI Perspective

AI Perspective adds a second analytical view after PlanSIP produces a result. It can consider the selected goal, risk level, time horizon and the historical information already shown by PlanSIP.

Math and data first

The AI features do not replace PlanSIP's calculators or historical analysis. PlanSIP first produces its normal calculation or data-based result, and AI is used to help explain or provide another perspective on that output.

Frequently asked questions

Is AI Assistant the same as AI Perspective?

No. AI Assistant is interactive and question-driven. AI Perspective is designed to provide a second analytical view of an existing PlanSIP result.

Does PlanSIP AI choose a fund for me?

No. PlanSIP is an educational analysis tool. Its AI features help explain and analyse information; they are not a substitute for personalized advice from a SEBI-registered investment adviser.

Does AI replace historical NAV data?

No. Historical data and PlanSIP calculations remain separate from the AI layer. AI is used as an additional explanation and perspective feature.
