What this calculator helps with

A SIP calculator helps estimate future corpus from monthly investment, assumed annual return, and investment period. It is useful for planning first crore goals, retirement, education, house goals, and long-term wealth creation.

What makes PlanSIP different

PlanSIP includes a simple SIP calculator for quick estimates and SIP Replay for historical mutual fund outcomes using actual NAV dates.

How to use it

  1. Open the SIP Calculator tab.
  2. Enter your monthly SIP amount.
  3. Choose expected return, years, and step-up.
  4. Review invested amount, estimated value, and growth path.
  5. Use SIP Replay to compare real fund history.

Important reminder

Estimated SIP returns are not guaranteed. Historical NAV simulation shows what happened in the past, not what will happen in the future.
