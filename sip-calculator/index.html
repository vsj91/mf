What this SIP calculator helps with

A SIP calculator helps estimate future corpus from a monthly investment, assumed annual return, and investment period. It is useful for retirement planning, education goals, first crore goals, house goals, and long-term wealth planning.

Step-up SIP calculator

The step-up field lets you increase SIP every year. For example, a 10% step-up means a ₹10,000 monthly SIP becomes ₹11,000 per month in year two, ₹12,100 in year three, and so on.

SIP formula

For a fixed monthly SIP, estimated future value is commonly calculated as FV = P x [((1 + r)^n - 1) / r] x (1 + r), where P is monthly SIP, r is monthly return, and n is total months.

SIP example

If you invest ₹10,000 per month for 15 years at an assumed 12% annual return, the calculator estimates the possible future corpus and separates total investment from estimated gain.

SIP calculator FAQs

Is SIP return guaranteed?

No. SIP returns depend on market performance and fund behavior. Calculator output is only an estimate.

Should I use fixed SIP or step-up SIP?

Fixed SIP is simpler. Step-up SIP is useful when you expect income to grow and want your investment amount to increase gradually.

Can this calculator select a mutual fund?

No. This page estimates SIP math. Use PlanSIP's mutual fund finder and SIP Replay only for educational fund study, not as buy or sell advice.
